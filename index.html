Direct-drive toolhead for the Geeetech A10M: a Bambu P1S/X1-style hotend, an Orbiter v2.0, 2× 5015, a BLTouch and an ADXL345 on an MGN12H rail. The part-cooling ducts were designed with CFD and the whole head was checked with vibration FEA. It has not been printed or measured yet.
